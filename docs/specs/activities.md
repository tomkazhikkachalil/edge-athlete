## Convention 30 — Activities are not sports: one normalized shape, parsed where it must be, the route private at both ends (Activities program, Sep 29 2026, #1003–#1008, mig 245 the ONLY DDL)

**Activities are not sports: one normalized shape, parsed where it must be, the route private at both ends (Activities program, Sep 29 2026, #1003–#1008, mig 245 the ONLY DDL)** — Tom: athletes bring what their watch or app recorded; phase 1 is FILE import (.FIT / .GPX / .TCX), providers (Strava, Google Health, Garmin) are later adapters onto the same `NormalizedActivity` (`src/lib/activities/types.ts`). `activities` (posture A) is one row per activity; `activity_type` is its OWN list (`catalog.ts` ≡ 245's CHECK), never a `SportRegistry` key, and phase 1 writes no `athlete_performances`. The dedupe key is the START SECOND (`fileExternalId`) — the same run as .FIT and .GPX is one activity. GPX/TCX parse in the BROWSER (Vercel's 4.5 MB cap; regex scanner, no DOMParser) into `wire.ts`; a .FIT uploads RAW to `/api/activities/fit` — **`@garmin/fitsdk` is SERVER-ONLY (its license: Garmin confidential, not to be made available to third parties; a test holds every import to a `-server` module)**. `write-server.ts importActivity` is the ONE writer (server-computed totals, a device total kept only when plausible, stream to storage BEFORE the row). `read-server.ts resolveActivityAccess` is the ONE gate (self / guardian → owner; departed, blocked, Only me → 404; `canViewProfile` or public signed-out) and `visibility.ts` the projection: a viewer's route is trimmed 200 m at BOTH ends (`trimStream`; `route_preview` is stored trimmed), a SUPERVISED athlete's viewers get no position at all (Tom's rules); every response `private, no-store`. Streams are gzipped JSON at `uploads/activities/<profile>/<id>.json.gz` — a PROTECTED prefix, removed by the delete and the deletion engine (`goes`). Share to feed: the posts route REBUILDS `stats_data {type:'activity'}` from the row (a client's is a request). **Activities are a section of TRAINING since Oct 1 2026** (they were their own tab): `src/lib/training/session-math.ts` is the one week rule over workouts AND activities (`TrainingSession`; the workout-only helpers delegate), `?tab=activities` opens Training at that section on BOTH profile routes, the Training "Workouts" privacy aspect hides the listing from a viewer, and a shared activity is a `training` post. **Connected apps (Oct 1 2026, mig 247 — PAUSED and hidden in PRODUCTION since Oct 2 2026, Tom: watch connections return with the native apps, `docs/ROADMAP_2026-10.md`; the flag stays on in CI, Preview and locally so the code keeps its tests):** a watch or app is connected ONCE in Settings → Connected apps (`NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_CONNECTED_APPS`, a surface flag) and delivers by itself — `activity_connections` (posture A, `goes`; ONE reader / writer `activities/connections-server.ts`, no response ever carries `secret_ciphertext` or `token_hash`), provider tokens sealed by `crypto/secret-box-server.ts` (`CONNECTIONS_ENC_KEY`, fail closed), `ACTIVITY_SOURCES` / `CONNECTION_PROVIDERS` ≡ 247's CHECKs with each provider's honest `stage` (Strava left out by decision — its terms forbid showing an activity to anyone else), ONE activity however many deliveries (`dedupe.ts`: a start within 60 s and a duration within 10% is the same activity, replaced only by a richer copy), a supervised athlete cannot connect. The Apple Watch path is the personal upload link (`POST /api/activities/inbound/[token]` — no session, the token is the authorization, sha256 at rest, shown once; `adapters/health-export.ts` is pure and never guesses). Polar is OAuth (`providers/polar*.ts`): a SIGNED state binds the callback to the session that started it, the webhook's signature is its gate (the payload's `url` is never fetched), a 401 from Polar marks the connection revoked, the terms' credit is `sourceCredit` ("Recorded with Polar", words not a logo) and the consent line is shown before the athlete leaves; a provider is offered only when the deployment holds its credentials AND the sealing key. The feed card is a line drawing (`route-svg.ts`, no Leaflet in the feed), Create → Activity. **The activity page's map is a FRAMED picture, never a free map (Oct 9 2026, `src/lib/activities/route-frame.ts`):** the box is computed on the client from the stream THIS viewer was given (never stored — a stored box would leak the trimmed ends), padded 25 % with a 400 m minimum span; Leaflet gets `maxBounds` + `maxBoundsViscosity: 1`, `minZoom` = the padded box filling the container (recomputed on resize), `maxZoom` 17, no zoom control (a "Fit route" pill), no scroll-wheel / box / keyboard zoom, the attribution kept, the OSM layer muted (`ea-route-frame` — on the WRAPPER, never on the Leaflet container: Leaflet owns that element's class list, and a state-dependent React className wiped it on the first Satellite switch, Oct 14 2026), reduced motion honoured; `data-map-*` on the container are the e2e hooks. Under Save-Data (`src/lib/net/reduced-data.ts`; the public site's live island keeps its own two-line reader because guardrail B4.11 pins its imports — the two are pinned equal by test) the page shows the line drawing with "Show map". `docs/ACTIVITIES.md` is the reference (the provider seam, Strava's owner-only rule); read DEVLOG Sep 29 2026 (Activities PR 1–6) first.
**Live Activities (Oct 4 2026, #1068–#1076, migs 250 · 251 — `docs/ACTIVITIES.md` "Recording live"):** a walk, run, ride, swim, row or any of 26 timed types (three groups, `recording: gps | timer | duration`) is recorded LIVE at `/activities/record` into THIS pipeline as `source 'live'` — never a workout kind. The screen owns no rule: `record/recording.ts` is the pure state machine (fixes admitted ≤ 50 m / ≤ 1.5 × maxSpeed, none while paused — a pause IS a gap; elapsed from clock deltas), `record/storage.ts` the IndexedDB draft (resume after a reload), the import door the recorder's door (`format: 'live'`, `external_id live:<recordingId>` — a retry never duplicates; segments re-clamped and STEPS ESTIMATED on the server, "est." — the web has no pedometer; a watch twin keeps the richer stream and the recorder's segments). A web page gets GPS only in the foreground with the screen on (wake lock; a hidden page is a gap the return toast names) — `ROADMAP_2026-10.md` §2's "not a recorder" is superseded. **Segments are boundaries, measured from the stream at read time** (`segmentStats`, never stored), reach every audience WHOLE (a time range reveals no place). **Photos** (`activity_media`, 216's shape): the owner's file only (`posts/<profile>/`), `at_s` → the pin is DERIVED from the VIEWER's trimmed stream (none inside the 200 m, none for a supervised athlete's viewers), mirrored into the post on share. **An activity is not a sport until the athlete POSTS it as one** (the share-time bridge, `sport-bridge.ts`): swim → swimming, ride → cycling, run → running, row → rowing — four `StatLinePostAdapter` sports on ONE endurance stat line (`enduranceSchema`; km for land, m for water; pace lower-is-better); the posts route builds the line from the ROW and `fromStatLinePost` writes the performance row. The gym path: the workout catalog has walk / hike / elliptical and `workoutToSession` carries `distanceM`. **GPS quality (Oct 9 2026, `src/lib/activities/gps-filter.ts`):** the recording keeps every RAW fix with its accuracy (`acc`; only stale and > 50 m are refused at the door); ONE pure, deterministic fold — `liveRoute` = refusals (the type's accuracy limit, stale, a jump measured against the FILTERED position) → a cold-start settle (3 fixes within 20 m, the route starts at their MEAN) → a constant-velocity Kalman filter per axis (measurement noise = accuracy², a manoeuvre boost) → emission only when confidently moving and > 6 SD from the last drawn point (between steps nothing; standing still repeats the last point — 0 m added) → a 5-sample elevation median — runs on the phone (the map, `liveTotals`, "Finding GPS…") AND in the writer for `source 'live'` (watch / file imports untouched), so the saved distance is the one on screen. The raw fixes are stored in the stream's `raw` block for re-processing and are SERVER-ONLY (`projectStream` strips them for every audience). Locked-phone recording is impossible for a web page — it is the native-app round. Read DEVLOG Oct 4 2026 (Live Activities PR 0–8) and Oct 9 2026 (GPS accuracy) first.

## Convention 45 — Every activity records what its kind of session records, asks at most three questions, and never invents a number (activities details program, Oct 16 2026, #1205–#1218, mig 265 the ONLY DDL)

Tom's review against Strava, Garmin, Apple Fitness, Hevy/Strong, Concept2, Slopes and Down Dog (the user's complaint: a yoga session recorded nothing a yoga session is). The rules, each detailed in the dated sections below: **tiles per type, only with a value** (`tiles.ts`, `derived.ts`); **the Finish asks effort 1–10, notes and up to three of the type's own questions, pre-filling CHOICES only** (`details.ts` zod-free + `details-schema.ts`; one `activities.details` object — a new question is never DDL; column-backed answers via `answersOf`); **a round timer read from the recording's clock**; **climbs with grades**; **the sport session** (`gps_optional`, never a result); **strength is the Workout** — records for every exercise derived from the sets (`workouts/prs.ts`) and effort on `workout_sessions` (`docs/specs/workouts-and-sessions.md`); **training load = minutes × effort over rated sessions, never imputed**. Units follow the athlete (`units.ts`). Combat sports with bout results are Convention 44 (`combat-sports.md`).

### Oct 16 2026 — The sport session (activities details A9, mig 265's columns)

- **`sport_session`** — a practice or a game tagged with its sport (Tom, Oct 15): the picker's own **Sport** group; recording mode **`gps_optional`** — a timer, with a map only when "Map my movement" is ticked before the start (`RecordingState.mapOn`, `recordsGpsFor(state)` — every "is this a GPS recording" reads the recording, not the type). It counts toward Training's week like any activity; it is **never a sport result** (`sportForActivity` → null, pinned; the bridge refuses it).
- **Its answers:** sport (required — every enabled registry sport, the combat four among them), practice · game · other, minutes played, position (behind More). The sport and the kind are **columns** (`sport_key`, `session_kind`, 265 — other readers query them across rows): a details field's `column` is now generic, `parseFinishAnswers` hands them to the writer, and `answersOf(type, row)` reads them back into the same answers shape for the page, the card, the edit form and the pre-fill (`last-details`). A session is never saved, or retyped to, without its sport (400 by name); retyped away, it loses both columns.
- **Shown:** "Soccer · Practice · 60 min played · Midfield" on the page and the card; tiles Time, Distance (only when mapped), Effort; Training's recent sessions show the sport's icon and "Soccer · Practice" under the name.

### Oct 16 2026 — Climbing with grades and sends (activities details A8, zero DDL)

- A climbing session is its climbs (`src/lib/activities/climbing.ts`, pure): each a grade on the session's scale, **Sent** or an **attempt**, tries; at most **50** (`details.climbs`, inside 265's 8,000-character `details`). Five scales, easiest → hardest: **V-scale** (VB–V17) and **Font** (3–9A) for bouldering, **YDS** (5.5–5.15d) and **French** (4a–9c+) for routes, and **gym colours** (white … orange). The style offers its own scales (`SCALES_FOR_STYLE`, a generic `filterBy` on a choice — the server refuses a scale the style does not offer, and the form drops one when the style changes).
- **Logged as it happens:** on the recorder, the climb log (scale first, then "Log climb" → a sheet: grade chips, Sent / Attempt, tries) dispatches into the stored recording (`climbScale`, `climbs`; `climbLogged` / `climbEdited` / `climbRemoved`), so a reload keeps them; each carries its second in the recording. Choosing the scale is offered only before the first climb.
- **Finish** carries them as `details.scale` + `details.climbs` beside the questions (where, style); the same log edits them there and on the activity page's edit form. The next session pre-fills where, style and scale — never climbs.
- **Hardest send** counts sends only; gym colours rank only when the athlete answers that their gym's colours run white → yellow → green → blue → red → purple → black (`colours_ordered`, under "Gym colours") — otherwise no Hardest send tile. Tiles: Time, Climbs, Sends, Hardest send, Effort; the page lists the climbs ("V4 · Sent · 2 tries").

### Oct 16 2026 — Yoga and the other session, snow and hike questions (activities details A7, zero DDL)

- Rows in the details table for **yoga** (style; heated, and the room's temperature under it; class or self-guided; behind More: focus areas and poses), **pilates** (mat or reformer, class or self-guided, focus), **stretching** (body areas, purpose), **HIIT** (format — Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP, intervals, circuit — pre-filled from the round timer when one ran, Rounds reading as Intervals; exercises behind More), **martial arts** (discipline, including the four combat sports; session; sparring rounds under "Sparring"; belt or rank), **dance** (style, class or freestyle), **ski / snowboard** (resort or backcountry), **cross-country ski** (classic or skate), **hike** (pack weight behind More).
- Session tiles: Time, **Rounds** (the round timer's `round` segments), **Work / rest** (the first round and the rest after it, "0:20 / 0:10"), **Sparring rounds** (only when answered), Effort, then heart rate and calories only when a device gave them. The answers read as the details line ("Vinyasa · Heated · 40 °C · Class · Hips").
- A ski or snowboard day shows elevation gain — as **Climbed** — only when it was backcountry; a resort day's gain is the lift's.

### Oct 16 2026 — The Finish asks effort, notes and the type's own questions (activities details A6, mig 265)

- **Effort 1–10** (`activities.effort`) on every Finish: two rows of five chips, each ≥ 44 px; tapping the chosen one clears it. **Notes** are sent at last (`activities.notes`).
- **One table** (`src/lib/activities/details.ts`, zod-free — the feed reads it): `DETAIL_FIELDS_BY_TYPE` lists each type's questions (`choice | multi | bool | number | text`). At most **three** show; the rest sit behind "More details"; a sub-question (`showIf` — a room's temperature under "Heated") goes with its parent, is not counted, and its answer is dropped when the parent no longer holds. Every answer is optional; nothing is invented. The server's parser is `details-schema.ts` (strict, refusing by name). A new question is a table row and a test, never a migration (`activities.details`, one jsonb object ≤ 8,000 chars).
- **Pre-fill: choices only.** `GET /api/activities/last-details?type=` (acting profile, `private, no-store`) returns the last activity of the type's choice / multi / yes-no answers (`prefillFrom`); numbers and text always start empty — last session's numbers are never saved as this one's. Effort and notes never pre-fill.
- **Units:** measured answers are stored metric and shown in the athlete's units (`units.ts`, from the km/mi setting): °C/°F, kg/lb; an imperial swimmer sees 25 yd first.
- **Columns, not details:** a row's stroke rate and watts and an indoor ride's cadence and watts fill `avg_cadence` / `avg_power` — only when no device gave one.
- **Shown:** an Effort tile, a Lengths tile for a pool swim (`pool.ts`; 25 yd = 22.86 m), the answers as one line on the page and the feed card (`detailLine`, viewer's units), the owner's edit form (a type change clears the answers). A pool swim posted as a Swimming result carries `pool_length_m`.
- Types in A6: pool swim, open-water swim, row, treadmill, indoor ride, elliptical, stair climber, paddle, skate.

### Oct 16 2026 — The round timer, Recent types, optional distance (activities details A3, zero DDL)

- **The round timer** (`record/round-timer.ts`, pure): five formats — Rounds (presets 3×3:00/1:00, 5×5:00/1:00, 12×3:00/1:00), Tabata (fixed 8 × 20/10), EMOM (a minute each, optional work window), AMRAP (one countdown; "+1 round" taps), Intervals (work/rest × N, optional warm-up and cool-down). Each is one `schedule` of phases. **The phase is read from the recording's clock** (`elapsedS` — timestamps, pauses excluded): the timer stores the recording second it started at, so a pause pauses it and a locked or throttled page never skews it; `boundaryCrossed` rings once on return for any bell missed. One timer per recording, kept in the stored recording (`timer`).
- **At Finish** its work phases become `round` segments ("Round N") and its rests `recovery` segments, clipped to where it stopped; an AMRAP's rounds are the taps. Past the 50-segment cap the rests go first. `'round'` is a code-only `SEGMENT_KINDS` entry (the DB CHECK holds the array and cap).
- **The bell** (`record/bell.ts`): a synthesized tone (no file) armed by the timer's Start tap, plus vibration where the phone has it (not iOS). The recorder already holds a screen wake lock while running; when it is not held, the timer says "Your screen may lock and stop the bell — keep it awake in Settings › Display" (Tom's review: a locked iPhone silences the bell).
- **Recent**: the picker's first row is the last six types used (`record/recent-types.ts`, device storage, `data-record-recent-type`); `data-record-type` stays unique per type.
- **Distance is optional** at Finish for elliptical, stair climber and indoor ride (`DISTANCE_OPTIONAL_TYPES`, `requiresManualDistance`).

### Oct 16 2026 — Per-type tiles and the derived stats (activities details program, A1, zero DDL)

- **Which numbers an activity shows is per type** (`src/lib/activities/tiles.ts`): each type belongs to a family (foot · trail · wheel · snow · water · indoor · session) that names its tiles in order. **A tile appears only when it has a value** (Tom's rule, Oct 16): no "—" placeholders, no Distance on a session type, no heart rate while no device supplied one, no "Moving time 0:00". The detail page shows at most 9; the feed card the first 4, the profile list the first 3 values.
- **Derived, never stored** (`derived.ts`, from the viewer's stream, once per fetch): best efforts (1 km · 1 mi · 5 km · 10 km; run, trail run), downhill runs (zig-zag with 30 m hysteresis; ski, snowboard), max speed (5-sample window, the type's ceiling ignored above), high point, climb rate (m/h over climbing time), grade-adjusted distance (Minetti cost curve over ≥ 20 m steps → "Grade-adjusted pace" on trail runs). `DERIVED_BY_TYPE` says which a type computes.
- Hikes, rides and mountain bikes show **Total time** beside Moving time whenever they differ; other types only past a minute. Ski / snowboard lead with Descent (the row's `elev_loss_m`), Runs and Max speed, never Elevation gain.
- **A row reads per 500 m** (`paceStyle: 'pace_500m'`); `format.ts paceMetres()` / `paceSuffix()` are the one reading of a type's pace (100 m swims, 500 m rows, the viewer's km/mi otherwise).
- The shared card's snapshot gains `elev_loss_m`, `max_speed_mps` and `runs` (optional — older cards read as before).

### Oct 21 2026 — The finish is two steps: the questionnaire, then preview and post (Training fixes PR 6, zero DDL)

- `FinishSheet` has a `step`: **details** (the route and the numbers, the name, a timer type's distance, the type's questions, Delete, **Continue** — `data-record-continue`) then **preview** (the feed switch, "post as", the caption, the feed's `ActivityPostCard` over `previewActivityPostStats` — the ONE composition `post-card.ts composeActivityPostStats` the server uses too — **Edit** `data-record-edit`, Delete, Post / Save). The step, the caption, the switch and "post as" live on the recording (`review`, `setReview`, finished only); the answers, the name and the distance reach it as typed. The URL mirrors the step (`?step=`, `pushState` / `popstate`): Back and Forward move one step; a reload returns to the step. Delete asks "Delete this activity? This can't be undone." Pinned by `activity-finish-steps.test.ts`; every activity spec clicks Continue before the preview's controls.
