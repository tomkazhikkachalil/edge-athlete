# The golf round setup as steps

> Added Oct 28 2026 (the golf round as steps, zero DDL). Read the devlog entry "October 28, 2026 — The golf round setup as steps" before changing any of it.

## Convention 53 — A golf round is set up in steps; every input the form had keeps its place; the frame is StepFlow and the list follows the first step

**Tom's spec (Oct 28 2026):** replace the one long golf round form with a step-by-step flow; every existing input, option and behaviour stays, only the way through them changes; back and forward never lose an answer; every step shows where you are; later sports reuse the frame. The review found eighteen things the form did that the spec did not name (rating/slope, the custom course's par & yardage, the multi-course club chooser, the worldwide search, the info card, Score Entry, the post's story, the draft review landing, the date↔mode flip, what is actually required, …) and four places it changed behaviour while saying it did not; Tom's four decisions are below.

**The door is unchanged.** Golf is still the composer's sport slot (`SPORT_COMPOSER_EXTRAS`, `GolfComposerSection`): Create → Sport → Golf, `/feed?create=1&sport=golf` (Log a round, the onboarding CTA), the Get Started card, General post → Golf in the picker — every one opens step 1. There is no standalone sport screen (Tom, Oct 13). Above the frame the sport row is ONE line while the steps run (`data-post-type-compact`, Oct 29 2026 — the big Post Type card took a third of a phone screen): the sport's name with **Change** (the picker) and **Remove sport**, the way back to a General post.

**The frame is `StepFlow`** (`src/components/StepFlow.tsx`, Program D's shell; `SportSetupFlow` was the first user). It gained three optional props for this program, all defaulting to the old frame: `refusal` (why Next is grey, beside the button — `data-step-refusal`), `finishTone="live"` (the red Go Live) and `busyLabel`. The finish honours `canAdvance` too. The composer's own footer, tags, hashtags, Tag People and the audience switch stay off while golf is the post type (the golf submit never sent them; the already-played round decides its feed on the draft review): the step footer is the ONE set of buttons, and the header's X is the close (`useDirtyClose` + `ConfirmModal`, as before).

**The list follows the first step** (`src/lib/golf/round-steps.ts`, zero imports; `round-steps.test.ts` pins every row):

| Mode | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| Outdoor (the default) | Round type · Course · Game format · Conditions · Players · **Start** (slide to go live) — 6 |
| Indoor | Round type · Course · Game format · Players · Start — 5 |

(**Nov 24–25 2026, Tom's part 2 spec, see the last section:** no round has a Scores step, reversing Oct 31 2026; "Already played" is not offered until its process is rebuilt, so every round started here is live. Decision 4 and the Scores / Preview notes below describe the flow as it was.)

The step is held by ID (`GolfComposerValue.stepId`, riding the crash draft so a restore lands where it was); an id the new list lacks starts over at step 1 (`roundStepIndex`). Conditions and Players are skippable — nothing on them was ever required. **Only the course step (and the finish) refuses Next**, for the two fields the form always required: a course (`REFUSAL_COURSE`) and, for an already-played round, the date (`REFUSAL_DATE`). Weather, temperature and wind carried asterisks on the form but were never enforced; the asterisks are gone.

**Tom's four decisions (Oct 28 2026):**
1. **Step 1 keeps today's defaults** — Playing now + Outdoor pre-selected, Next available at once (the spec's "nothing pre-selected" was dropped).
2. ~~Near me on the course step runs on entry when location is already granted~~ — **superseded Oct 30 2026 (Tom's follow-up): search first, Near me on demand, in every mode.** The course step opens with the search field first, empty, focused only behind a fine pointer (the sport picker's rule — a phone shows no keyboard until the tap); nothing location-based happens until "Near me" is tapped; then the nearby list and the "Playing at X?" offer as before. `geolocationGranted()` is gone; Convention 34's "on a TAP only" stands without exception.
3. **The date is locked to today while Playing now** — the date control shows only for an already-played round (default today, `max` today); choosing Playing now sets today (`roundDateForMode`). The form's past-date → Already-played flip is retired.
4. **A Scores step for Already played only** — the multi-player grid and the hole-by-hole Quick entry (`ScoreEntryModal` batch mode), as the form had them. Playing now has no scores step: scores are entered on the live page. Finish → create → `/athlete/drafts/[postId]` → Post, as before (Convention 33).

**What each step holds (every control the form had, moved verbatim):**
- **Round type** (`RoundTypeStep`): "Golf" (and "for {athlete}" when a guardian composes), Playing now / Already played with the mode's help line, Outdoor / Indoor. Indoor clears weather, temperature and wind (the form used to submit values entered before the switch).
- **Course**: the date (already played only); the search field with catalog browse on focus, typeahead, the km chips, "Search all courses worldwide" (the only provider trigger), the unavailable row, attribution, Escape closing the dropdown first; Near me; the "Playing at X?" offer (live only); the selected-course badge; the multi-course club chooser and the two-nine "Pick your back nine"; `CourseInfoCard`; the custom-course notice; Tees (outdoor only — a catalog course's real tees hardest first, **each named with its yardage and rating / slope** (`teeChoiceLabel` over `courseSummary`, Oct 29 2026: "White · 6,412 yds · 71.2 / 128", a part only when the course knows it), else the five colours as names; a pick re-fills rating/slope and yardages); Course Rating / Slope Rating (once a course is named; auto-filled, editable); the custom course's Par & Yardage table.
- **Game format** (`FormatStep`): Stroke Play / Stableford / Match Play and the two help lines.
- **Conditions** (`ConditionsStep`, outdoor only, skippable): weather, temperature, wind — optional, said so. **Weather at the course (Oct 29 2026):** for a LIVE round (Playing now) at a catalog course with coordinates the step offers **Use the forecast at the course** — one tap asks OUR route (`GET /api/weather?lat=&lng=`, the course's 2-decimal cell; the browser never meets the provider) and fills the three fields from Open-Meteo's current block (`golfConditionOf`, `golfWindBandOf`, `golfTempF` in `src/lib/weather/weather.ts`: rain beats everything, the strong band reads as windy, snow and fog as cloudy; the mph bands are the select's own). The fields stay editable (a typed value marks the conditions `manual`); the line under the grid says what came and credits the source ("Weather by Open-Meteo", CC-BY); the finish summary's Conditions row says "Forecast at tee-off". An already-played round never sees the button, and switching to Already played or Indoor forgets the forecast. The temperature shows in the viewer's unit (`unitSystemOf(readUnitPreference())` — the Units row in Settings → Account) while `golf_scorecard_data.temperature` / `golf_rounds.temperature` keep their Fahrenheit meaning: the conversion lives at that one edge. The provider: Open-Meteo's FREE host (non-commercial) until go-public; `OPEN_METEO_API_KEY` (dual-purpose with elevation) flips to the paid host; `WEATHER_OFF=1` is the brake; an IP bucket (`weather`, shadow) and a provider day budget (`provider:weather`, 8,000) stand in front; every failure is a quiet "No forecast right now" — on screen weather is never an error. Pinned by `src/lib/weather/__tests__/*`, `weather-surface.test.ts`, `e2e/golf-forecast.spec.ts` (the stand-in `e2e/helpers/weather-mock.ts`).
- **Players**: the card as it was — Add Others (`TagPeopleModal`), chips with Remove, the creator always on the card and always inserted server-side; a removed player loses their score row.
- **Scores** (already played only): the Score Entry card — Quick entry + `MultiPlayerScorecardGrid`.
- **Start / Create**: a summary (`data-round-summary-row=…`: course, date, round type, format, tee, conditions, players), the mode's line; for an already-played round the composer's media + caption block (`finishSlot`) and a Preview button; the footer's finish is Go Live (red, pulsing) or Create Post. `onFinish` IS the composer's `handleSubmit`: `submitSharedRound` and `afterCreatePath` are untouched and the request body is the same.

**Unchanged by design:** no 9/18 or Front/Back picker (derived at submit from the holes scored); `isValid` = course + date; `isDirty` as before (a seeded row is not work); the crash draft (`ea:composer-draft:v1`) carries the golf value while dirty, now with the step; the hole-range and creator-row render-phase syncs.

**Pinned by:** `round-steps.test.ts` (the four lists, skippable, index fallback, the refusals, the date rule, the labels), `step-flow.test.ts` (the refusal line, the finish's `canAdvance`, the tones), `request-position.test.ts` (granted only); e2e `golf-quick-entry.spec.ts` (step 1's Live / Already played keys and the hold on Already played, Nov 27 2026; the already-played walk to the draft review left with the Scores step on Nov 25 2026; the defaults, the indoor list and the clean close; **Playing now to Go Live through the UI** — the first spec to; the phone Quick entry walk with no overflow on any step), `composer-near-me.spec.ts` (the course step's auto-run where the Permissions API answers granted, the tap elsewhere), `header-create`, `composer-default-general`, `sport-favourites`, `dropdown-visibility` (the frame is the door; the course field is step 2).

### The live path's look (Oct 29 2026, Tom's refinement spec — #1359 the account unit, PR B the look; zero DDL in PR B)

Tom's second spec refines the look and copy of the **Playing now + Outdoor** path; Already played and Indoor are a later pass. **The numbering** is that path's list and stays in code, tests and specs: `LIVE_OUTDOOR_STEPS` in `round-steps.ts` (pinned equal to `stepsForRound(DEFAULT_ROUND_ANSWERS)`): **1** Round type (`type`) · **2** Course (`course`) · **3** Game format (`format`) · **4** Conditions (`conditions`) · **5** Players (`players`) · **6** Start (`finish`).

- **Purple is the one selected colour** (Tom's decision 3: selection states plus the chosen-course badge, the Near me chip and the "Playing at X?" offer row; heading icon accents stay green for now): Playing now / Already played, Outdoor / Indoor and the three formats press `bg-brand text-white` with a violet hover on the unselected twin; the badge (`data-course-chosen`), the chip and the offer row wear the house light-purple pill (`bg-brand-soft` + `border-violet-200 dark:border-violet-800`); the search spinner is `border-brand`. **Step 1's dot** (`data-live-dot`) inside Playing now is red (pulsing) while it is selected, white when it is not.
- **Step 2:** `CourseInfoCard` lost the "View on map" Google link; two equal buttons under the summary — **Show Map** (`data-course-show-map`) and **Official Scorecard** (`data-course-show-scorecard`) — open ONE panel at a time directly below (`open: 'map' | 'scorecard' | null`; the summary's map thumbnail and `defaultOpen` open the map; `mapMode="hidden"` callers never render Show Map); the open one reads **Hide**, `aria-expanded`. `CourseScorecardTable` (server-renderable, shared with the public org pages): Par and HCP rows `border-y-2 border-border-strong` (`data-course-scorecard-row`), the HCP row on `bg-brand-soft` with its sticky label cell painted the same. **One row that fits, the sums emphasised (Oct 30 2026, Tom's follow-up — replacing the stacked nines of the same morning):** Hole · 1–9 · **Out** · 10–18 · **In** · **Tot** in compact `px-1` cells (an eighteen fits the composer's card at desktop width; the earlier `px-2` row ran to ~950 px in ~800 and was cut off); Out / In / Tot are boxed (`border-l-2 border-r-2 border-border-strong`, the left border off the last hole) and bold (`data-course-scorecard-sum="out|in|total"`; a nine ends in Total); the tee's rating / slope suffix rides the label from `sm`; a phone swipes sideways with the sticky label column. **Honest states (Oct 30 2026, Tom's empty panel on a real course):** the button is offered only when the course HAS a sheet or may still get one — a catalog row that is not map-only (`source !== 'osm'`; OSM rows never gain holes) whose detail has not answered; a map-only course gets no button (the summary's "Not fully mapped" says it) and a detail that answers without holes drops the button and closes the panel; the panel is the table, the skeleton (`data-course-scorecard-loading`) or the line "No official scorecard for this course yet" (`data-course-scorecard-none`) — never nothing behind Hide. The account unit is adopted only when the SERVER value changes (`lastAdopted` in `useUnitSystem`). **Find the official scorecard (Oct 31 2026, Tom on The Marshes):** a catalog row with no sheet and none coming (a thin provider row whose own provider had nothing, or a map-only OSM row) offers **Find the official scorecard** (`data-course-find-sheet`) in the scorecard slot: a signed-in tap asks `GET /api/golf/courses?id=&sheet=1` → `findOfficialSheet` (course-catalog.ts) asks GolfCourseAPI by NAME, `pickSheetCandidate` keeps the ONE same-facility candidate agreeing on country / region (the city deciding between several; two that fit = no match), and the detail's pars, handicaps, tee yardages and ratings are written onto THAT row (identity, coords and source kept; `hydrated_at` stamped). Found → the panel opens and the composer adopts the fuller row (`onCourseUpdated` → `applyCourseData`); nothing → "No official scorecard found for this course yet" (`data-course-sheet-none`). Budgeted (`golfcourseapi`, two hits), rate-limited with the search. The labels read **Tees** / **Tee Color**, **Course Rating**, **Slope Rating** (no "(optional)"), the two inputs side by side; the "18-hole rounds … unlock your estimated handicap" paragraph is gone.
- **Step 4:** the "Optional — skip if you'd rather not say" line is gone (Skip in the footer says it). A **°F / °C** toggle beside the Temperature INPUT (Nov 19 2026: in the label row it stood twice as tall as the other labels and pushed the box ~20 px out of line with Weather and Wind on web; the label row is now the label alone) (`data-golf-temp-units`, two `aria-pressed` pills `data-golf-temp-unit="°F|°C"`) chooses the ACCOUNT's unit system (Tom's decision 1: °C = Metric, °F = Imperial — the one preference Settings → Account offers, migration 293 `profiles.unit_system`, `PATCH /api/settings/units`, `useUnitSystem`: the device at once, the account in the background; `UnitSystemApplier` seeds the device from the profile on every load). The field re-renders in the chosen unit; the stored value stays Fahrenheit.
- **Step 5:** the heading is **Playing Partners** (no icon); the "Playing with anyone? Optional (n)" line is gone; **Add Others** is the purple CTA (`data-round-add-players`) over the same `TagPeopleModal` (which gained the `unoptimized` guard its avatars needed for Google pictures); an added player is a ROW (`data-round-partner=<id>`): `AvatarImage` 32 px (initials fallback), the name, Remove, on the light-purple pill.
- **Step 6:** the Conditions row is ONE line — `conditionsLine` in `round-steps.ts`: the weather word without its emoji, the temperature tight in the viewer's unit ("7°C"), the wind band as its mph range (`windRange`: 0–5 / 5–10 / 10–20 / 20+ mph) — "Forecast at tee-off" lives on the step's note, the round card and the round page, not here. The Players row is a stacked LIST (`data-round-summary-row="players"`, one `data-round-player=<id>` each): the creator first, then every partner — picture, name, and ` · index 12.4` when the handicap is known (Tom's decision 2: EventPlayers' wording, `handicapSuffix`, `formatHandicapIndex`). Pinned by: `round-steps.test.ts` (the numbering, `conditionsLine`, `windRange`, `weatherWord`), `handicaps.test.ts` (the parser, the suffix, the route's contract), `unit-prefs.test.ts` (the account unit); e2e `golf-quick-entry.spec.ts` "the live path look" (purple, the dot, the two buttons one at a time with the purple HCP row, no "(optional)", the toggle landing on the account and showing in Settings, Playing Partners) and the already-played walk's players list, `golf-forecast.spec.ts` (the one-line summary), `course-preview.spec.ts` (the thumbnail still opens the map).

**A card before any score (Oct 31 2026):** an unfinished round's full scorecard shows every hole (`scorecardHoleNumbers`, round-display.ts) on any course, sheet or not; the preview includes the creator and badges an already-played round correctly; a catalog course with no sheet offers the Par & Yardage table (round-only, the catalog row is untouched); hydration never overwrites an existing sheet with an empty answer.

**Scores on every round, and the backfill (Oct 31 2026, Tom):** this reverses decision 4: Playing now has a skippable Scores step too, so the live + outdoor path is 1 Round type · 2 Course · 3 Game format · 4 Conditions · 5 Players · 6 Scores · 7 Start, and Preview is on both finishes. The batch backfill was removed the same day, before any batch ran: GolfCourseAPI's terms ban harvesting a copy of its data. A provider card is only ever asked for when a person picks the course (`autoFindSheet` in the composer, one ask per course per day); no bulk caller exists.

**The scorecard from the map (Oct 31 2026, Tom):** a course with no provider card takes its card from its cached map lines at read time (`withMapSheet`, `map-sheet.ts`): only with exactly 9 or 18 consecutive holes, each with a real par and a line. One measured yardage per hole under the `map` tee, no handicaps or ratings, labelled "from the course map, not the official card"; a provider card always wins; nothing is written to the catalog. Manual par entry is NOT offered on catalog courses (Tom); it is removed from the #1372 addition.

**A map card can become a real card (Oct 31 2026):** picking a map-card course also asks the provider, silently. A found card replaces it only with the same hole count and at most 2 differing pars (`sheetAgreesWithMap`); otherwise the map card stays and the refusal is logged.

**Estimated pars where the map has lines but no pars (Nov 18 2026, Tom, reversing the Oct 31 "no estimates" rule for this one case):** a course with all 9 / 18 lines drawn but a par missing on any hole gets a card too (`sheetFromGeometry(…, { estimate: true })`): a tagged par is kept, a missing one is `estimatePar` of the hole's measured length (`PAR_CUTS_YARDS` 240 / 460 / 720, fitted on production's 8,522 fully tagged courses: 93.8 % of holes right, 45 % of courses entirely right, 75 % within one hole), and each such hole carries `parEstimated`. The course reads `sheetSource: 'map_estimated'`; the table says "Pars marked * are estimated from the hole's length" (`data-course-scorecard-map="estimated"`), its button reads **Scorecard** (not Official), the score grid's row reads **Par (est.)**, and the mark rides into the round's `hole_data`. **An estimate is never evidence:** `readMapSheet` (what `findOfficialSheet` and the background fill compare a provider's card with) reads tagged pars only, so any provider card replaces an estimated one and no map is recorded as overruled. The pick tracker counts it under its own kind, `estimated` (migration 315). **The saved round keeps the mark (Nov 19 2026):** the posted scorecard reads **PAR (est.)**, the scorer's header, the live page's hole chip and the group score card read "Par N (est.)" (`roundParsEstimated` / `holeParEstimated`, `src/lib/golf/holes.ts`); the profile copy keeps it per hole in `golf_holes.par_estimated` (migration 316, written by the round mirror), shown on the round detail page, the round card and the golf profile. Still not offered: a player typing pars on a catalog course.

**The tracker (Oct 31 2026, migration 294):** each pick in the composer counts the course's card kind in `golf_course_picks` (no user ids, QA accounts excluded), shown by `GolfCoursePicksPanel` in the admin console: the list any future data spend should target.

**A club found to be several courses during the lookup (Nov 19 2026).** When "find the official scorecard" answers `split` (see `golf-course-data.md`), the course step shows the club chooser at once, without a "nothing found" line; choosing a course makes it the selection and its own card the round's. A row that is itself a named course of a club is a selection, not a question; a course whose length is not yet known shows no hole count in the chooser.

### The mechanical dialect: the first four screens (Nov 23–24 2026, Tom's "Golf Round Flow: Visual Design Spec" — #1619, #1620, #1621; zero DDL)

Tom's spec restyles four screens — **Select sport**, **Round type** (step 1), **Course search** and **Course selected** (step 2, swapped in place): "modern with a few mechanical touches. Recessed slots, raised keys, small engraved style labels, fixed width numbers. Keep text to a minimum." It is a **visual change**: no behaviour, data, route or step order changes, and every control is wired to the handler the form already had. The palette, the classes and the scope's rules are in `ui-conventions.md` ("The mechanical dialect"). Steps 3 to 7 keep their bodies and inherit the palette through the scope; their look is a later pass.

**Tom's decisions (Nov 23 2026):**
1. **The spec's dark palette exactly, plus a light twin** (the spec said "dark theme only"; the app has two themes and the sport picker is shared by every sport).
2. **Built:** the Men | Women tee switch, Recent courses, the LAST tag. **Not built:** the 18 / F9 / B9 switch (9 or 18 is still worked out from the holes scored); "favourites listed once" was not built then; **built Nov 25 2026** (one home per sport, `ui-conventions.md` → the sport picker).
3. **Rating and slope fields are dynamic** — "make it dynamic so it follows real data": typed only where the card cannot answer.

**The shell.** While golf is the post type the composer wears `.ea-mech`: a full screen on a phone, a centred dialog from 640 px whose width follows the step (`roundFrame` in `round-steps.ts`): **480 px** on the round type and the course search, **760 px** once a course is shown and on every later step, **920 px** while the picked course's scorecard is open (an eighteen with its tee rows does not fit 760; a wide table gets a layout that fits, never a hidden sideways scroll). `StepFlow variant="mech"` (golf only — `SportSetupFlow` and `NewVideoFlow` keep the house frame) draws the sport row (`lead`: Golf · Change · Remove, still `data-post-type-compact` / `data-post-type-clear`) and "STEP n / N · LABEL" on one row, one segment per step, Back 92 × 48 and Next 132 × 48, and a `footerCenter` slot. The words "Step n of N · label" stay in the text (`sr-only`) for readers and specs. Skip, the refusal line and the live-red Go Live are unchanged. General and stat-line posts keep the composer as it was.

**Select sport** (`SportSelector`): a tile grid — 3 across on a phone, 5 from 640 px — icon top left, name bottom left, the favourite as a **switch** top right (a sibling button with the star's exact contract: `aria-pressed`, the two fixed labels, `aria-describedby`, `data-sport-favourite`). Since Nov 25 2026 a favourite has ONE tile: it moves between the two sections at once with a 200 ms slide (`ui-conventions.md` → the sport picker). SOON replaces the switch on a sport that is off. **LAST** marks the sport of the account's newest sport post (`GET /api/settings/last-sport`, `private, no-store`, its own limiter bucket; `lastUsedSport` — derived, never stored).

**Round type** (`RoundTypeStep`): two `KeySwitch`es (real `aria-pressed` buttons with the same names). The helper sentence is behind an info button (`InfoToggle`) that swaps the first switch for a panel of the same height (64 px), so nothing below moves. The broadcast dot is the live red, lit while Playing now is the pressed key (`data-on`). The indoor note stays as one soft line ("Indoor rounds skip the playing conditions." since Nov 27 2026: an indoor round picks a tee). The sport is named in the sport row, so the "Golf" heading is gone; a guardian's "For {athlete}" line stays.

**Course — two views of one step** (`GolfComposerSection`; `courseShown = selectedCourse || (manualCourse && a typed name)`):
- **Search.** A 48 px recessed slot whose text IS the round's course name (as the field always was — a typed name lets Next on) and the 48 px **Near me** key (still the only place the location is asked, only on its tap; `aria-label` carries the three states). The results, the "Playing at X?" offer, the worldwide-search row, the attribution and the failed-search line are **one list panel in the flow** (`data-course-results`) — nothing floats, so nothing is clipped or scrolled into view. **RECENT** (`data-course-recent`) lists the catalog courses of the account's own latest rounds until a search or Near me takes the place: `GET /api/golf/courses?recent=1` reads the caller's group posts' `golf_scorecard_data` and their `golf_rounds`, merged by time (`recent-courses.ts`, five distinct); anonymous → empty; derived, never stored. A typed name shows **Add course manually** (and "No course found" once an answer came back empty for that text): the door to the picked-course view for a custom course.
- **Picked course.** `CoursePlate` (the club's logo by its own website through Logo.dev — the golf glyph with no token, no website or a failed load; name; place or "Custom course"; **Change**, back to the search), `DialCluster` (Yards · Rating · Slope under an "18 HOLES — PAR 72" strip; `dial.ts`: a 270° sweep, linear, clamped, a resting needle and a dash for no value; "est." when pars are estimated, "≈" for a map-only yardage), **Men | Women** (only when the course has both sides: the catalog marks a women's box by the " (f)" key suffix, so that is what the switch splits; rows drop the "(F)"), `TeeTable` (the old Tees select's options as rows; a row's tap runs the select's handler — tee, that tee's rating and slope, the per-hole yardages; a tap on the picked row lets it go), rating / slope **slots** only where `ratingEntryNeeded` says the card cannot answer (a typed course, an unrated row, a tee without both numbers), and `CourseInfoCard variant="mech"` — the SAME card (one panel at a time, the honest states, the auto lookup, the club-split report) drawn as the utility row **Map · Scorecard · Website**. Indoor: no tee table and no switch. The club chooser, the custom course's par and yardage table and the already-played date keep their places. From 640 px the four blocks are two columns (plate, dials, utility row | switch, tee table); an open panel takes the full width under both. The Logo.dev credit sits between Back and Next while a logo is shown.
- **Indoor (Tom's indoor course spec, Nov 27 2026; migration 322).** An indoor round's course step is the same view with four differences. (1) **The tee table is there** — the ONE `TeeTable` and `pickTee`, never a second list — **without Men | Women**: its rows are `indoorTeeKeys` (the men's boxes; a course that has only women's boxes shows those — Tom's call, not the profile's gender). A women's box picked before the switch to Indoor is let go. (2) **Next waits for a tee** (`roundStepRefusal`: "Pick a tee first"; a name typed but not yet added reads "Pick a course, or add the one you typed"). The finish never asks for it. With no tee the round carries NO rating or slope (it used to save the white tee's numbers for a tee nobody chose). (3) The dials' strip says only **Par** (`showHoles={false}`; holes are worked out from the holes played — there never was an 18 / F9 / B9 switch) and the utility row has **no Website** (`hideWebsite`). (4) **The putting rule** (`PuttingRule`, between the tees and the utility row; the left column on a desktop): a three-way `KeySwitch` — **Putt out** (every putt counts) · **Gimme** (a ball inside N ft is holed for 1 putt) · **Auto 2** (any ball on the green is holed for 2 putts) — with green lamps (`lampTone="good"`: green is this control's signal, apart from the selection purple), an `InfoToggle`, and a readout of ONE height (52 px) that shows the mode's words, the gimme distance slider (`RangeSlider`: `role="slider"`, 1–15 ft, step 1, default 6, a 44 px row, drag by touch or mouse, arrows / Page / Home / End; "Gimme distance in feet"), or — info open — one line for the mode in force. Nothing below it ever moves.
