# The pulse — the header's numbers in one call (Convention 54)

**Convention 54.** **Program:** the cost program (Oct 29 2026, `docs/COST.md`). **Status:** PR B shipped (migration 292); migration 300 (Nov 5 2026, the Videos program Phase 4a) added the fourth number, live broadcasts; PR C (the change signals, migration 301, Nov 6 2026) SHIPPED.

## The problem it solves

Every signed-in visible tab needed two numbers on every page — is anything live (the header's Live dot, `useLiveNow`, mounted by the header AND the tab bar) and how many messages are unread (the bell, `MessagesProvider` in the root layout). They were three polls: `/api/golf/live-now/count` + `/api/sport-events/live-now?count=1` once a minute and `/api/messages/unread-count` every two minutes. Three Vercel function calls a minute per tab, each with two to four Supabase trips, for every user, all day — the single largest sustained cost in the app, and the line that would make 10k users cost hundreds a month for nothing a user sees.

## The rules

1. **One endpoint.** `GET /api/me/pulse` → `{ liveNow: { golf, events, broadcasts, total }, unread: { messages } }`, `private, no-store`, `requireAuth`. One RPC, `me_pulse(viewer)` (migration 292; 300 added `live_broadcasts` — the published linked YouTube broadcasts with `live_status = 'live'` the viewer may see under the owner's own rule, the mirror of `GET /api/videos/live-now`; `docs/specs/videos.md` Phase 4a), the SQL mirror of the routes' pure rules: golf = `countLiveVisibleRounds` / `isRoundLive` (`type='golf_round'`, status pending | active, the round's DATE within ±48 h of now read as UTC midnight, public OR the viewer on the roster with any status but `declined`); events = `readLiveEvents` / `liveEventCards` (`status='live'`, public OR the viewer's accepted row, AND a round with `status='live'`); messages = `get_unread_message_count` as it is. The three old routes stay (the strip's list, the parity spec, the load scripts). A deploy ahead of the migration answers 503 `Pulse updating`.
2. **One store.** `src/lib/pulse/pulse-store.ts` is a module-level store (the shape `useLiveNow` had): one coalesced fetch, one timer per tab, one resume catch-up, subscribers opt in — never a context provider (that would mount a fetch for signed-out visitors on public pages). It is the ONLY place that fetches `/api/me/pulse`. `useLiveNow(enabled)` and `MessagesProvider` subscribe; the header's count and the bell's total are the same fetch.
3. **When it fetches** (`pulse-rules.ts`, pure): never hidden or offline (the return catches up); a mount or a return reuses an answer younger than 30 s; the timer (10 min since PR C — a net under the signals; a time-only transition such as the 48 h window lapsing lags at most that long), a signal (debounced 1.5 s + up to 3 s jitter so a platform-wide tick spreads) and a demand always fetch. A failed fetch keeps the last numbers — the numbers are a nicety, never an error state.
4. **Local corrections, no fetch.** A conversation read here subtracts its unread from the store (`adjustPulseUnread`), never below zero; logout resets the store. The provider's optimistic decrement and the store agree.
5. **The list is not the count.** The conversation LIST has no poll (PR C): the `new_message` bell is its signal for a message from someone else (scale fix 3), a FETCHED pulse whose unread number moved refreshes page one (a muted conversation, a read on another device — `fetchedAt` tells an answer from a local correction), and the return catch-up covers the rest. Own sends from another device reach the list on the next of those.

## The change signals (PR C, migration 301, Nov 6 2026)

6. **The database says when.** Seven triggers broadcast through `realtime.send` (`public.pulse_send`: one event `pulse`, the payload plus `at`, every error swallowed — a signal never fails a write). Two topics, both PRIVATE since 304 (the hardening pass, Nov 6 2026 — the public topic was spoofable: any client with the anon key could broadcast a `pulse` and make every tab fetch): **`live-now`** (a SELECT policy for `authenticated`, no INSERT policy, so only `pulse_send` writes) — a golf round entering or leaving pending | active, a sport event or an event round entering or leaving `live`, a broadcast entering or leaving live + published; payload `{kind, at}` and nothing else, because who may see what stays server-side: every client answers by fetching its OWN pulse. **`user:{uuid}`** (private; the `realtime.messages` SELECT policy `realtime.topic() = 'user:' || auth.uid()`) — a message in one of my conversations (every active, unheld participant, the SENDER and muted participants included — the two gaps the bell has), a read on any device, my own seat on a live private round. No publication change, no new `postgres_changes`.
7. **Two channels per tab, one socket.** The notifications channel IS the private topic (`src/lib/notifications.tsx`: `user:{id}`, `config.private`, the JWT bound by `supabase.realtime.setAuth` in auth.tsx) and carries its two notification listeners plus the pulse broadcast; `src/lib/pulse/PulseHost.tsx` (mounted in the (app) layout, signed-in only) holds the `live-now` topic (`config.private` too). A broadcast → (a signal fires no sooner than `SIGNAL_FLOOR_MS` = 5 s after the last fetch — `signalDelayMs(random, sinceLastFetch)`, defence in depth behind the private topics)  `requestPulse('signal')` (1.5 s + up to 3 s jitter, a burst = one fetch); a (re)SUBSCRIBED → `requestPulse('resume')` (an answer younger than 30 s is reused). The timer is a 10-minute net; the conversation-list poll is gone; `LiveNowStrip` refetches its lists when the pulse's live total changes (a guest, who has no pulse, every five minutes). Per visible tab at rest: ~0.1 pulse calls a minute, down from 3 before PR B.
8. **What lags.** A time-only transition (the 48 h window lapsing, a lazy auto-end that writes nothing) shows within 10 minutes; a soft-deleted message, a hold or a leave likewise (by decision — the net covers them). A dead socket is caught by the heartbeat, the rejoin fetches.
