# Calendar: events and repeating series

> The calendar's rules were only in the devlog until Nov 6 2026 (Jul 28 2026 "calendar" and Aug 29 "series zones" entries). This file starts with the one rule that round changed; extend it as later rounds touch the calendar.

## Convention 58 — A repeating event's schedule changes from one event on, as a split (Nov 6 2026, migration 302)

Every occurrence of a series is a real `events` row (057/058, at most 104, a never-ending series six months ahead through the daily cron); the series row (`event_series`) holds the RULE only — no anchor of its own: `extendRecurringSeries` (`src/lib/calendar/series-server.ts`) steps from the EARLIEST row's `starts_at` and `timezone`. RSVPs, reminders, carpools, posts and contests hang on occurrence rows. So a changed rule or zone is never a rewrite of the rows in place; it is a **split**, what the old copy told people to do by hand, in one tap:

- **The route:** `PATCH /api/calendar/events/[id]` with `scope: 'following'` and a `recurrence` (the POST shape) and/or a changed `timezone`. Any other scope answers 400 ("…changes from one event on — choose This and following events"); guests change in their own save (400 otherwise); a single event never takes a rule (400). The math stays in TS: `planSeriesSplit` (`src/lib/calendar/recurrence.ts`, pure, tested) re-anchors the occurrence's WALL CLOCK in the new zone (a 7 pm practice stays 7 pm at the venue), takes the new rule or carries the old one (`carriedRecurrence`: an until as the chosen day in the new zone, a count as the count still to come, never below one) through `validateRecurrenceInput`, and generates the new occurrences.
- **The persistence — ONE transaction**, `calendar_series_split` (302, service_role only, the organizer checked): the old series ends at the anchor (`ends='until', until_at = the anchor's start`); its active occurrences at or after the anchor are **cancelled, never deleted** (overridden ones included — they belonged to the old schedule; a post or a contest keeps pointing at its cancelled row); the new series row, its occurrences and the anchor's guest rows (status, reminder preserved) are inserted. The answer is the new first occurrence's detail plus `split: { new_series_id, cancelled, created }`; ONE "updated a recurring event" notification per guest. A deploy ahead of the migration answers 503 `no-store`.
- **The form** (`EventFormModal.tsx`): on a series event the rule and the zone sit behind **"Change schedule…"**; opened, the create-time repeat controls (prefilled from the stored rule, "Does not repeat" withheld — ending a series is Cancel this-and-following) and the zone picker appear under the line "The new schedule starts with this event. Earlier events keep the old one."; Save offers that one scope.

Pinned by `src/lib/calendar/__tests__/recurrence.test.ts` (the planner), `src/lib/__tests__/calendar-series-split.test.ts` (302's shape, the route's gate, the form), e2e `calendar-series-split.spec.ts` (six weekly → every two weeks from the 3rd: 1–2 stay, 3–6 cancelled, four new rows with the guest; then a zone-only change keeps 7 pm) and `calendar-timezone.spec.ts` (the form's door on a phone).
